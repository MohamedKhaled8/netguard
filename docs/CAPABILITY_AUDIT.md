## Detailed Technical Notes on Non-Feasible Items & Honest Alternatives (Item D9)

1. **Intra-browser WebRTC socket QoS:**  
   - *Technical Limitation*: Modern browsers (Chrome, Edge, Firefox) multiplex WebRTC audio, video, data channels, and regular HTTP/HTTPS traffic through internal thread pools and shared network sockets. An external OS QoS policy cannot inspect encrypted WebRTC frames inside Chrome's memory to prioritize voice over video.  
   - *Implemented Honest Alternative*: When WebRTC activity or microphone usage is detected in a browser, NetGuard assigns `TrafficPriorityClass.REALTIME` to the entire browser process, elevating its outgoing packets to DSCP 46 while simultaneously throttling background cloud uploaders (OneDrive, torrents) to preserve upload headroom.

2. **Routerless external device traffic control:**  
   - *Technical Limitation*: Without administrative access to the home router, NetGuard runs as client software on the local PC and cannot shape or drop packets originating from other physical devices on the Wi-Fi network (e.g. smart TVs, family phones).  
   - *Implemented Honest Alternative*: NetGuard utilizes high-precision local QoS to ensure THIS PC's packets have absolute priority over local buffers. Additionally, the pre-flight check and real-time health evaluator detect when bottlenecks originate externally (local gateway latency elevation vs internet loss) and advise the user honestly without false blaming.

3. **ISP uplink capacity guarantees:**  
   - *Technical Limitation*: NetGuard cannot force the Internet Service Provider (ISP) to provide more physical bandwidth or prevent ISP central office congestion.  
   - *Implemented Honest Alternative*: Early warning detection identifies packet loss or jitter escalation within <= 3 seconds and provides actionable mitigation advice (e.g. "أغلق الكاميرا للحفاظ على استمرار الصوت") while restricting local background uploaders to 1 Mbps.
